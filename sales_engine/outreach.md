# Outreach scripts

Same rules as TenderFind:

- Reply to every enquiry within 1 hour.
- No cold WhatsApp messages to strangers. No scraped numbers.
- Post in Facebook groups only with admin permission.
- Never promise a win.

**POPIA and direct marketing.** As I understand it, POPIA section 69 lets you approach someone *once* by email or message to ask for consent to market to them. You may also market similar services to your own existing customers, as long as every message lets them opt out. Have a lawyer or the Information Regulator's guidance confirm this before a big campaign. Everything below stays inside that: one polite consent-asking first message, existing clients, in-person visits, public posts and inbound replies.

---

## 1. Existing TenderFind clients (start here: warmest, allowed)

Anyone who bought a Tender-Ready Pack, CSD/CIDB help or Bid Help.

**WhatsApp**
> Hi [name], Jay from TenderFind. Quick one: before you submit your next bid, I can now run it past a panel that reads it the way an evaluation committee does. You get a list of gaps (missing documents, weak sections) within 24 hours, plus rewrites of the weakest parts. R1,500 for an RFQ. Want me to check the next one you're working on? Reply STOP if you'd rather not get offers like this.

## 2. Facebook tender groups (value first, with admin permission)

**Post**
> The 5 things that get bids thrown out before anyone reads the methodology, from what I see every week:
> 1. Tax compliance PIN missing or expired
> 2. SBD forms unsigned or the wrong version
> 3. CSD number not on the forms
> 4. CIDB grade lower than the tender asks for
> 5. "Experience" with no references the department can phone
>
> Check these 5 before you submit. If you want a second pair of eyes on a bid, I do a 24-hour Bid Check. Happy to answer questions here.

Then answer questions in the comments. Move to WhatsApp only when *they* ask.

## Replying to "Will this help me win?"

> It won't promise a win. Nobody honest can. What it does is find the gaps an evaluator would find, so you fix them before you submit, not after you're disqualified.

## Replying to "Is my document safe?"

> I remove ID numbers, phone numbers and emails before it's processed. I use Anthropic's Claude service, and I delete your documents within 30 days. I need your written OK first; a WhatsApp message is fine.

Only say this once you have checked Anthropic's terms (see offers.md).

## Replying to "That's expensive"

> An RFQ Bid Check is R1,500. If it finds one missing document, that's a bid you would have lost without anyone reading it. Start with one and judge it yourself.
