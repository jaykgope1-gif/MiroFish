# Offers: Buyer & Evaluator Stress-Tests

Sold under TenderFind SA. One business, one WhatsApp number (069 289 8507), one sprint.

## What the client gets

A report within 24 hours that shows how a panel of simulated evaluators (for a tender) or buyers (for a campaign) reads their document:

- a scorecard per criterion, and how many of the panel would shortlist / act on it
- possible disqualifying issues (missing returnable documents, unsigned forms, no tax PIN, wrong CIDB grade)
- the 5-8 fixes that matter most, in order
- **Before vs After**: rewrites of the weakest passages, scored again by the panel
- every fact the client must confirm before using a rewrite, marked `[CLIENT TO CONFIRM]`

Made with `sales_engine/stress_test.py`. Model cost per test is about R3-R10; the budget cap is R50.

## Words to use and to avoid

Never promise a win, a higher win rate or a conversion rate. The panel is an AI simulation; it finds gaps fast, it does not predict outcomes.

| Say | Don't say |
|---|---|
| "Find the gaps an evaluator would find, before you submit" | "Win-rate package", "boost your win rate" |
| "A second pair of eyes, overnight" | "Guaranteed to score higher" |
| "Shows which parts are weak and how to fix them" | "Predicts how the committee will score you" |

This is why the R20,000 offer is called *Tender Bid Desk Partner*, not *Tender Win-Rate Package*.

## Client consent (POPIA)

Your own rule is that client documents never go into AI tools. This service needs them to. So before any test, get written consent. WhatsApp or email is fine. Wording:

> I agree that TenderFind SA may process the document(s) I send for this stress-test using Anthropic's Claude AI service. Personal details such as ID numbers, phone numbers and email addresses are removed before processing. TenderFind SA deletes my documents and the report from its own systems within 30 days of delivery unless I ask for longer. I can withdraw this consent at any time.

Then pass how you recorded it to the tool: `--consent "WhatsApp consent, T. Mokoena, 12 Nov 2026"`. The tool refuses to run without it.

Before you sell this, read Anthropic's commercial terms and data-retention policy. Only promise clients what those terms say. If a client says no to AI processing, offer your normal manual Bid Help instead.
